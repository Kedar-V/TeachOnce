## Authenticity notes

- **The inference claim comes from the app, not the terminal.** The result banner reads "Generated by live SFT vLLM (NVIDIA-Nemotron-Nano-12B-v2-VL-BF16-SFT) with guided_json". That string exists only in `teachonce-web/mock-server/app/vllm_backend.py`, the live path after `POST {VLLM_BASE_URL}/chat/completions`.
- **The terminal is the Flowstate backend log (uvicorn :8000), not vLLM.** Green lines are the frontend polling `GET /api/studio/jobs/job_0a941148f5`, and the on-screen footer says so. The bottom strip is labelled "ARCHITECTURE · analysis condensed (real run ≈ 40 s)": it's a diagram, not a log.
- The execution shown (EX-SFT-ESCALATE-0025, Severity Escalation) is an existing run, not a run of the newly generated workflow. Validation and publish are real and bridge the two.
- DAG: other paths aren't dimmed or animated on real footage. The graph movement is the app's own recorded zoom and pan, plus eased camera framing.
