# SOP-CS-214 — Laptop Warranty & Service Resolution

**Owner:** Customer Support Operations  
**Audience:** L1 / L2 support agents  
**Effective:** 2025-03-01  
**Classification:** Internal

---

## Purpose

Resolve laptop hardware complaints consistently and in line with warranty policy. Do not create unauthorized repair orders.

## When to use

Customer reports a hardware fault on a company-supported laptop (keyboard, display, battery, motherboard, etc.).

## Operator brief

1. Open or confirm the support case.
2. Look up the customer and confirm the device on the case.
3. Check warranty coverage for that device.
4. Review available diagnostics for the reported fault.
5. Resolve according to coverage:
   - If the device is under warranty and the fault is covered → create a repair request.
   - If warranty has expired or the fault is not covered → record paid service options for the customer.
6. Update the ticket and verify the final state before closing.

## Notes

- Escalate to a human reviewer when CRM, warranty, and diagnostics records conflict.
- Do not invent serial numbers or override warranty status without approval.
- Retry failed tool calls once; then escalate.

## Out of scope

Parts fulfillment, severity / on-call paging, and RMA logistics use separate procedures.
